Research Collection Mode

Collected by the author of this repository (github.com/silvamleandro) to evaluate a technical case in a hiring process. This is not an Incognia collection. Recorded: technical characteristics of the browser, such as screen, GPU, language and time zone, and the dynamics of the interaction, such as timings, pointer trajectories and the category of each key. Not recorded: the content of what you type or your IP address. The participant code comes from your invitation link, or is random when the link has none, and only groups your sessions. The data is deleted when the hiring process ends; to delete it sooner, send the author your participant code. Nothing is sent until you click the button.
